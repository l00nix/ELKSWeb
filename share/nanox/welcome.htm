WebNX - Nano-X browser for ELKS

This experimental PC-98 browser uses Nano-X for graphics, keyboard and mouse. It requires a Nano-X server and runs separately from the direct ELKSWeb browser.

Browse: press Ctrl-L, enter a hostname or HTTP address, then press Enter. Use Tab and Enter to follow links. Press Escape to leave.
